About the data

These figures come from the Texas Real Estate Research Center's second-quarter 2026 rural land market report. They are published regional or statewide price-per-acre indicators, not county-level comparable sales or appraisals. The calculation is acreage × published benchmark; it is not a parcel-specific valuation.

Smaller and larger tracts may sell for very different prices per acre. This version does not automatically retrieve tract-size-specific figures, county sold comps, days on market, or utility values. Checked improvements are recorded but do not change the price.
